The models behind your endpoint

Keera Gateway knows eight model providers: four in Switzerland, one in the EU and three in the USA. Here is every model they serve, with its context window and its price in CHF per million tokens. You decide which team may use which model, and pay for usage at the provider's list price.

Models that are not in the catalogue

Keera Engine runs any open model vLLM can serve, your own fine-tune included - on Swiss GPUs or in your cluster. We quote its price on request.

Any other endpoint that speaks the OpenAI API works too: another provider, or a server in your own data centre. You then enter its price yourself, so your budgets count it.
