Pricing · Keera Gateway

Start free, grow with your volume

Keera Gateway comes in three plans. In the Swiss Cloud you pay no base fee, only for model usage. Dedicated and Enterprise give you a gateway of your own.

Start for free See Keera Gateway ➔

Three ways to run Keera Gateway

Swiss Cloud runs on a shared instance at a Swiss cloud provider. More control, groups from your directory, a data centre you choose: then your company gets an instance of its own. The price is per organisation, not per user.
