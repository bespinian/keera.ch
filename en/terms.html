Prices and payment

All prices are in Swiss francs, plus VAT. Base fees are per organisation and due in advance, monthly or yearly; a yearly contract takes 10% off the base fee. Model usage is charged at the provider's list price, as shown in the model catalogue.

On Swiss Cloud, you pay for usage in advance as credit, through PostFinance Checkout. Each request is taken from the credit; once it is used up, requests to those models are refused. Credit does not expire and is not paid out, unless we end the contract through no fault of yours. Invoices are payable within 30 days.
